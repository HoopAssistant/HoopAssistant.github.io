What we do for the club

Three jobs, one taxonomy

Opponent, own games and training are tagged with the same logic and land in the same hub. The staff defines the categories; we apply them to every possession.

01

Opponent scouting

We cut four games of the opponent Löwen Braunschweig will face and tag every offensive and defensive action. Every clip arrives already segmented, so the staff opens the film and each action is classified.

Offense tagging

  • The call of every play
  • Play type
  • How the action ends: two point make or miss, free throws, foul, turnover
  • The player who finishes the action

Defense tagging

  • Pick and Roll actions and the zone of the court where they are played
  • Indirect screens, back screens, handoffs, zone defenses
  • Pick and roll coverage: plug and hedge, flat, under, switch...
  • The players involved, to know who switches

What the staff receives

  • Staff video of about 20 minutes with the most relevant offensive and defensive actions, clearly ordered
  • Player video of 5 to 6 minutes, cut from the same film
  • “How to Attack” video: offensive proposals built from watching the four games
  • Extra Nacsport lists: clips per player and per pick and roll coverage
02

Own games

Your own game is tagged with everything above, plus the specific things the staff asks for. It is back within six hours of the final buzzer, with the focus set by the staff before tip off, collective and individual.

Extra tagging asked by the staff

  • Paint touches per action and the type of each one
  • Ball reversals in every action and how they happen
  • Tag up of the defensive balance after a long or a short shot

What it feeds

  • Individual player videos
  • Post game video
  • Team offensive efficiency by situation: possessions, success rate, points and points per possession
03

5x5 training

Everything we do in games, we also do in training. On top of that, the club’s priority is its defensive balance, so we measure it in every 5x5 drill of every session of the season.

Tagged on every attack of every 5x5 drill

Ball reversalsNumber of reversals in each attack
Paint touchesNumber per attack and the type of each one
How it endsThe result of the action
Tag upWhether it happens or it is forgotten
Offensive reboundWhether there is an offensive rebound option

Defensive balance metrics

  • Is there balance or not, measured on every attack
  • Metrics for each item above, built by the hub and open to coaches and analysts
  • Individual progression you can show a player, not just describe

Delivery

  • Nacsport timeline of the session, cut and tagged
  • Returned before the next session, so correction happens while the rep is still fresh
